## 📋 3. Detailed Initiative Breakdown

### Initiative 1: Branch-Assisted Digital Onboarding & Literacy Program
- **Supporting Empirical Evidence:** 43.2% of Regular retail customers (29,300 clients) and 78% of senior depositors (aged 65+) do not actively use digital or mobile banking channels.
- **Proposed Management Action:**
  - Equip top 5 urban retail branches with self-service "Digital Concierge Setup Kiosks".
  - Train branch representatives to guide customers through mobile app download, biometric login, and initial digital bill payment.
  - Deliver in-app gamified micro-tutorials rewarding initial digital transactions with fee waivers.
- **Affected Business Area:** Retail Branch Network & Digital Channels.
- **Target Customer Segment:** Regular Retail Customers & Senior Depositors (56+ age bands).
- **Expected Benefit:** Reduce routine in-branch teller transaction volume by **20%**, cutting branch operating costs while boosting retail deposit stickiness.
- **Measurable Success Indicators:**
  - Regular Segment Digital Penetration: Increase from **56.8% to 71.8% (+15%)**.
  - Digital First-Time Active Users: **10,000+ new active mobile users**.
- **Monitoring Period:** 6-Month continuous evaluation with monthly cohort tracking.

---

### Initiative 2: Dynamic 2FA & Online Behavioral Fraud Ringfencing
- **Supporting Empirical Evidence:** Online Banking accounts for **70.2%** of all flagged fraudulent activity (2,110 cases out of 2,976 total portfolio fraud events), generating financial losses and customer friction.
- **Proposed Management Action:**
  - Implement automated device-fingerprinting and behavioral biometric velocity scoring.
  - Require mandatory out-of-band push-notification two-factor authentication (2FA) for all online transfers exceeding **$1,000**.
  - Establish a real-time transaction-hold queue triggering immediate SMS/Push confirmation for atypical cross-border transfers.
- **Affected Business Area:** Fraud Risk Operations & Information Security.
- **Target Customer Segment:** All Online & Internet Banking Users.
- **Expected Benefit:** Prevent **30%** of online fraudulent transfers, yielding direct loss savings of **$180,000+ in Year 1**.
- **Measurable Success Indicators:**
  - Online Fraud Incidence Rate: Reduced below **0.05%** of transaction volume.
  - False-Positive Authentication Interruption Rate: Maintained under **1.5%**.
- **Monitoring Period:** Weekly risk monitoring with quarterly threshold calibration.

---

### Initiative 3: Automated Provisional Credit for Low-Value Card Disputes
- **Supporting Empirical Evidence:** ATM, card issues, and transaction disputes drive **46.0%** of all complaints (5,315 tickets). Complex back-office investigation causes average dispute resolution to drag out to **7.6 business days**, directly precipitating a **>35% drop in subsequent customer transaction velocity**.
- **Proposed Management Action:**
  - Configure automated provisional credit approval for cardholder transaction disputes under **$250** upon ticket submission for verified active accounts.
  - Decouple customer reimbursement from multi-week interbank card network chargeback workflows.
  - Re-debit accounts only in verified cases of customer error or substantiated merchant fulfillment.
- **Affected Business Area:** Card Services & Customer Dispute Operations.
- **Target Customer Segment:** Active Debit and Credit Cardholders.
- **Expected Benefit:** Compress customer-perceived resolution time from **7.6 days to <24 hours** (<3 business days formal ticket closure), protecting customer trust and eliminating post-dispute card abandonment.
- **Measurable Success Indicators:**
  - Dispute SLA Compliance: Maintained above **95.0%**.
  - Post-Dispute Transaction Retention: **+8% increase** in card transaction activity following dispute settlement.
- **Monitoring Period:** 6-Month pilot on Visa/Mastercard portfolios with bi-weekly SLA audits.

---

### Initiative 4: Automated Wealth Management Liquidity Sweeps
- **Supporting Empirical Evidence:** TrustWave Bank's balance portfolio is heavily right-skewed. Upper-quartile accounts maintain balances exceeding **$104,230**, and the 18,600 Premium customers average **$214,500** per account, representing over **$9.5 Billion** in vulnerable liquidity reserves.
- **Proposed Management Action:**
  - Launch the "TrustWave SmartYield Sweep" feature within online and mobile banking.
  - Automatically sweep idle checking balances exceeding $50,000 into short-term treasury money market instruments, earning competitive yields while preserving same-day liquidity.
  - Introduce dedicated relationship-manager review triggers for accounts maintaining balances over $250,000.
- **Affected Business Area:** Wealth Management & Treasury Asset-Liability Management.
- **Target Customer Segment:** Premium & Upper-Quartile Balance Depositors (Q3 threshold >$104K).
- **Expected Benefit:** Defend core deposit liquidity against yield-seeking neobank migration and generate an estimated **$1.2 Million in annual fee-based wealth management revenue**.
- **Measurable Success Indicators:**
  - Premium Segment Active Deposit Retention: Maintained above **98.5%**.
  - Total Assets Under Administration (AUA) Swept: **$500 Million+ within first 6 months**.
- **Monitoring Period:** Monthly portfolio balance monitoring and quarterly churn tracking.

## 🏛️ 5. Steering Committee Governance Structure
To ensure cross-functional execution and accountability, the proposed initiatives will report to a **TrustWave Customer Engagement Steering Committee** meeting bi-weekly:
- **Executive Sponsor:** Head of Retail Banking & Digital Transformation.
- **Operations Lead:** Head of Customer Support & Dispute Resolution.
- **Risk Lead:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Fraud Operations.
- **Analytics Lead:** Lead Data Scientist / Business Intelligence Specialist.
- **Review Cadence:** Bi-weekly operational reviews with a formal 90-day executive checkpoint to decide bank-wide expansion.
