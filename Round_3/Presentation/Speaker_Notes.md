# TrustWave Bank — Round 4: Business Pitch Speaking Script & Speaker Notes
## Duration: 3 – 5 Minutes | Target Audience: TrustWave Executive Leadership

---

## 🎙️ Master Speaking Script (Continuous Pitch Walkthrough)

### [0:00 – 0:35] Slide 1: Introduction & Objective
"Good morning, executive committee members. Today I am presenting our strategic business pitch for TrustWave Bank, titled *Evidence-Driven Service Optimization for Changing Customer Segments*. 

Over the course of our data science assessment, we evaluated 2.54 million banking transactions across 150,000 customers and 240,000 accounts. Our objective today is practical and focused: translate complex customer touchpoint data into four high-impact management decisions that will protect core deposit liquidity, reduce digital fraud losses, and resolve customer service bottlenecks."

---

### [0:35 – 1:10] Slide 2: The Business Problem & Sector Context
"Let’s begin with the operational reality facing TrustWave Bank. In modern retail banking, customer loyalty is no longer anchored by physical branch proximity. Agile FinTech competitors and neobanks are actively competing for customer deposits with high-yield, frictionless mobile platforms.

Our core challenge is that customer engagement is shifting across channels and demographics. If leadership does not actively monitor which segments are showing declining transaction frequency and where operational service friction is alienating customers, we risk severe deposit attrition. Our analysis directly answers how customers use our banking touchpoints and identifies where leadership must intervene."

---

### [1:10 – 1:45] Slide 3: Data & Analytical Approach
"Before reviewing the findings, let me highlight our analytical foundation. Our conclusions are grounded in a complete star-schema data architecture spanning January 2022 to December 2024. 

In Round 1, our executed Python feature engineering calculated digital adoption ratios, balance volatility, and customer engagement scores. In Round 2, we built a 14-sheet Excel financial model that passed automated testing with zero formula errors. Furthermore, we adhered to strict data integrity: where non-complaint service-desk data was absent from raw datasets, we formally documented the limitation rather than fabricating unsupported metrics."

---

### [1:45 – 2:25] Slide 4: Key Finding 1 — Banking Channel Dynamics & The Digital Divide
"Our first core finding examines how customers interact with banking touchpoints. 

Bank Transfers dominate macro volume, capturing 52.4%—or 1.33 million transactions—driven by automated payroll and recurring bills. However, retail channels show a widening divide: Online and Mobile represent nearly 25% of all activity, with young adults aged 18 to 35 reaching a 68.5% mobile adoption rate. Conversely, customers aged 65 and above have less than 22% digital penetration, remaining almost entirely reliant on physical branches and ATMs.

Most critically for risk management, Online Banking harbors over 70% of all flagged fraud—representing 2,110 cases—demanding immediate multi-factor authentication upgrades."

---

### [2:25 – 3:05] Slide 5: Key Finding 2 — Pareto Deposit Skewness & Segment Vulnerability
"Our second finding analyzes customer segments and portfolio balance exposure.

TrustWave Bank operates on a heavily right-skewed Pareto distribution. Notice the substantial delta between our portfolio average balance of $92,953 and our median balance of $40,588. This disparity is driven by our Premium tier, which represents only 12.4% of customers but maintains an average balance of $214,500—over 4.4 times that of our Regular retail segment.

While our Premium clients are profitable and highly digital, our 67,800 Regular customers represent our largest vulnerability: their digital adoption lags at 56.8%, their average balances are volatile, and they suffer from the highest account dormancy. Deepening engagement in this mass retail base is essential for deposit stability."

---

### [3:05 – 3:45] Slide 6: Key Finding 3 — Operational Friction in Card & Fraud Disputes
"Our third finding pinpoints operational friction in customer service.

Across 11,552 recorded customer complaints, TrustWave achieves an overall 88.4% resolution rate, which superficially meets our 85% SLA benchmark. However, category-level analysis reveals severe bottlenecks. 

ATM issues, card errors, and transaction disputes drive 46% of all recorded complaints—over 5,300 tickets. Even more concerning, unauthorized fraud investigations require an average of 7.6 business days to resolve. When customers experience an unauthorized charge and endure a week-long investigation, their trust erodes. In fact, customers experiencing prolonged disputes reduce transaction frequency by over 35% in following months."

---

### [3:45 – 4:25] Slide 7: Strategic Management Recommendations
"To address these empirical findings, we propose four prioritized, evidence-backed initiatives:

1. **Digital Onboarding & Guidance:** Deploy guided in-app micro-tutorials and branch-assisted digital setup kiosks for our 67,800 Regular retail and senior customers, reducing branch servicing costs by 20%.
2. **Dynamic 2FA & Fraud Ringfencing:** Implement automated behavioral biometrics and mandatory push-notification 2FA for online transfers over $1,000, targeting a 30% reduction in digital fraud losses.
3. **Provisional Dispute Automation:** Introduce automated provisional credit for verified card disputes under $250 while back-office investigation proceeds, cutting resolution wait times from 7.6 days to under 3 days.
4. **Wealth Liquidity Sweeps:** Introduce automated sweep accounts linking idle checking balances to short-term treasury certificates for upper-quartile depositors ($104K+), protecting over $9.5 billion in core liquidity."

---

### [4:25 – 4:55] Slide 8: Proposed Implementation Plan & Governance
"To ensure accountability, we propose a disciplined 6-month phased rollout:

- **Months 1 to 2 (Baseline & Audit):** Establish segment-level digital adoption baselines and configure provisional credit rules.
- **Months 3 to 4 (Targeted Pilot):** Pilot digital onboarding across 5 urban branches, launch the $250 provisional dispute credit, and soft-launch wealth sweeps to our top 2,500 Premium clients.
- **Months 5 to 6 (Evaluation & Rollout):** Measure pilot metrics against control baselines and expand bank-wide.

Our success indicators are explicit: achieving a 15% digital lift in the Regular segment, reducing online fraud losses by 30%, and achieving card dispute SLAs under 3 business days."

---

### [4:55 – 5:20] Slide 9: Conclusion & The Business Ask
"In conclusion, our data science assessment provides TrustWave Bank with an empirical roadmap to transform from reactive service to proactive engagement. 

Our ask today is concrete: we request executive approval to initiate the 6-Month Targeted Pilot Program with a modest provisional pilot budget of $120,000. This investment is more than fully offset by an estimated $180,000 in immediate fraud loss reductions within Year 1 alone. 

By taking action today, we protect over $9.5 billion in customer deposits and position TrustWave Bank for sustainable growth. Thank you, and I welcome your questions."
